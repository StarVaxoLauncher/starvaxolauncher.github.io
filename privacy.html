Privacy notice for StarLauncher’s account, Discovery and theme-sharing features, operated by StarVaxo.

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Contact: starvaxo.studio@gmail.com

A theme approved for Discovery is intended for public sharing. The private storage channel protects unpublished files from public browsing; it does not make a published theme private.

Google sign-in and account information

We use Google sign-in through Supabase Auth to identify your account and secure community actions. Google supplies an account identifier, email address and basic profile information, such as your name and picture. Authentication records may retain this information. We do not request access to your Gmail messages, Drive files or contacts.

Your email is used for account identification and support; it is not included in public creator profiles. Google account information is not automatically used as your public creator name or picture. You can choose your public profile details separately.

What you choose to make public

Your creator username, display name, selected avatar, banner, profile colors and creator level can appear publicly. Discovery listings include theme titles, descriptions, tags, previews, creator information, ratings and download totals. Approved theme files are available to community users. Pending submissions and their previews are restricted to their creator and authorized moderators through our service.

What can be inside a theme

A .SV file can contain the sections selected when it is created: wallpaper, home-screen layout, app-drawer settings, icon appearance and other launcher preferences. Selected preference sections can include hidden-app identifiers and app targets assigned to gestures. Check your selections before exporting or submitting a theme. Account sign-in tokens are stored separately and are excluded from theme exports.

Applying a theme lets you select sections and decide whether to use the creator’s included preferences or keep your own. Sharing a file creates copies that recipients may retain.

Community activity and moderation

We store submissions, review status, account-linked votes, download records, upload quota records and profile changes to operate Discovery, count activity, enforce limits and moderate content. Download totals use authenticated account records. Moderators can review submitted themes and approve or reject them. Creator levels are based on approved submissions.

Service providers and storage

Supabase provides authentication, the community database and server functions. Theme and image assets are encrypted by our server using AES-256-GCM before storage in a private Telegram channel. Keys and bot credentials remain on the server. This is not end-to-end encryption: the service operator can decrypt stored assets, and authorized recipients can receive shared files.

Google, Supabase, Telegram and hosting providers process information needed to deliver their services, which may include request metadata and operational logs. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add advertising, analytics scripts or remote font services. Providers’ own processing is described in the Google privacy policy, Supabase privacy policy, Telegram privacy policy and GitHub privacy statement.

How we use Google account data

Google account data supports authentication, account security and account-related support. We do not sell it, use it for targeted advertising or share it for unrelated marketing. Public profile information and theme content are handled according to the sharing choices described above.

Security on your device

The Android app stores community session credentials in encrypted storage backed by Android Keystore, outside launcher theme exports and normal backup storage. No storage system guarantees absolute security. Avoid putting passwords, private documents or sensitive personal information inside shared themes.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Account, community and stored asset records are retained until removed by the operator. Rejected or failed submission assets may remain in private storage; quota and moderation records may also remain for service administration. Automatic deletion periods are not currently implemented.

You can sign out of the community and revoke StarLauncher’s Google access in your Google account. To request account deletion, theme removal, correction or information about your data, email starvaxo.studio@gmail.com from your account address. These requests are handled manually; there is currently no in-app deletion control. We may need to verify the request. Previously downloaded copies and provider backups may persist beyond removal from Discovery.

Updates

We will update this page when the features or data practices change and revise the date above. Contact StarVaxo with questions before sharing content you consider private.
