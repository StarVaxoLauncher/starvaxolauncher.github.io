These terms describe use of StarLauncher’s community profiles and theme-sharing service, operated by StarVaxo.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Your account

Use your own Google account and keep it secure. Choose a creator profile that does not impersonate another person or organization. You are responsible for content submitted through your account.

Your creations

Only upload wallpapers, artwork, icons and other content that you own or have permission to distribute. You retain your rights to your original content. By submitting a theme, you permit StarVaxo to store it, review it and, if approved, display its listing and distribute the theme to community users.

Approved themes can be downloaded and applied by other users. Downloading does not transfer copyright or grant permission to resell or redistribute third-party artwork. Respect any creator-provided license and applicable rights.

Keep private information out

Check the selected sections before submitting. Layouts and preferences can reveal app identifiers, hidden-app choices and gesture targets. Do not upload credentials, private documents, sensitive personal information or material belonging to someone else without permission. Published files can be copied; removing a listing cannot recall copies already downloaded.

Submissions and review

Each account can reserve up to ten theme submissions per UTC day. Failed and rejected submissions can count toward that limit. Submissions remain pending until a moderator approves or rejects them. Approval is not guaranteed. Use accurate titles, descriptions and category tags.

Do not submit unlawful content, malware, scams, harassment, sexual exploitation, or material that violates others’ rights. Do not manipulate votes, downloads or quotas. StarVaxo may reject or remove content and restrict accounts to address abuse or service problems.

A service in development

Features, limits and availability may change. The service is currently in testing and may experience errors or interruptions. Keep your own copies of themes and settings. Review theme contents and application choices before changing your launcher setup.

Contact and removal requests

Send account, content or copyright concerns to starvaxo.studio@gmail.com. Include the theme or profile involved and enough information to understand your request. Account and data handling is explained in the privacy notice.
